# Course Withdrawal

## Deadlines
A course withdrawal must be submitted by the deadline posted on the [ASU Academic Calendar](https://students.asu.edu/academic-calendar) for that term — typically the Friday of the 12th week of a full-length fall/spring session (earlier for 7.5-week sessions). After that date, withdrawal requires a petition and is not guaranteed.

## Steps
1. Log in to [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu) and go to **My Classes**.
2. Select the course you want to withdraw from and choose **Withdraw**.
3. Confirm the withdrawal — this replaces your grade with a **W**, which does not count toward GPA but does count toward attempted credit hours.
4. If you're on financial aid, check with Financial Aid before confirming: dropping below full-time status can affect eligibility.
5. Withdrawal is final for that term once confirmed; you cannot re-add the course after this point.

## The four things people get wrong
- Withdrawing does **not** refund tuition after the add/drop period — refund percentage is tied to the deadline you withdraw by, not the withdrawal itself.
- A W still shows on the transcript and counts toward the **maximum-attempts** limit for repeated courses.
- International students on an F-1/J-1 visa should confirm minimum full-time enrollment with ISSC before withdrawing — this can affect visa status.
- If the course is a prerequisite for something already registered next term, withdrawing can silently drop you from the downstream course — check your schedule after withdrawing.

You do not need an advising appointment to withdraw. You do need one if you're unsure how the W affects a specific degree requirement or your financial aid — that's where this stops being a form question.
