# Change of Major

## When you can do it yourself
If the major you want is **open enrollment** (not competitive/capacity-limited), you can change it directly:
1. Log in to [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu) → **My Programs** → **Request a Change of Major**.
2. Select the new major/college and submit. It usually processes within a few business days and does not require an appointment.

## When it needs an advisor
Some majors (e.g. many Engineering, Nursing, Business majors) are **restricted** — they require minimum GPA, specific prerequisite courses completed, or a formal application. For these:
1. Check the major's admission requirement page on the [ASU Degree Search](https://degrees.apps.asu.edu) — look for "change of major requirements," not just the general catalog description.
2. If you meet the stated requirements, the change-of-major request in My ASU will usually still go through, but restricted majors can flag for manual review.
3. If you don't yet meet the requirements (e.g. still finishing a required prerequisite), the request will be denied and you'll need to work with an advisor on a plan to become eligible.

## Before you request the change
Run the DARS "what-if" audit for the target major first (My ASU → **My Program** → **What-If Analysis**) — it shows how your completed courses would apply under the new major, which is usually the actual question behind "should I switch."

## The advisory case
If the real question is *"is this even the right major for me,"* that's not a form — it's worth talking to an advisor with your DARS what-if in hand. A bot can tell you what would transfer; it shouldn't be the one telling you whether to switch.
