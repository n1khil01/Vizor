# Requesting a Transcript

## Official transcript
1. Go to [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu) → **My Academics** → **Order Official Transcript** (this hands off to the National Student Clearinghouse).
2. Choose electronic (PDF, usually available within minutes once processed) or paper (mailed, takes longer).
3. There's a small per-copy fee. A **hold on your account** (financial, judicial, or advising) blocks transcript release — the order page will say so; clearing the hold is a separate step from placing the order.

## Unofficial transcript
Available free, instantly, in My ASU → **My Academics** → **View Unofficial Transcript**. Use this for your own planning — it's not accepted for most external submissions, which require the official version.

## Common snag
Grad school and employer portals often want the transcript sent **directly** from ASU, not uploaded by you. When ordering, choose the electronic delivery option and enter the receiving institution's email or portal address exactly as they specify — a transcript emailed to yourself instead of the institution gets rejected by most application systems.
