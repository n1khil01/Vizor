# Academic Standing / Suspension Appeal

## When this applies
If your ASU GPA falls below a 2.0, you're placed on **academic warning**, then **probation**, then **suspension** if it doesn't improve — the exact thresholds and terms are on your DARS report's GPA block. Suspension means you cannot register for the next term unless the suspension is successfully appealed or served.

## Steps to appeal
1. Log in to [My ASU](https://my.asu.edu) → **Academic Standing** → **Appeal Suspension** (only visible once you're actually suspended — it won't show for warning/probation).
2. Write the appeal statement: what caused the drop (be specific and honest — documented circumstances like illness, family emergency, or unexpected work hours carry more weight than a general "I struggled"), and a concrete plan for the next term (reduced course load, tutoring, specific support resources).
3. Attach supporting documentation if you have it (medical note, counselor letter, etc.) — not required, but strengthens the appeal.
4. Submit before the stated deadline for the upcoming term; late appeals are rarely reviewed in time to register.

## What actually gets appeals approved
Appeals committees are looking for a plan, not just an explanation. "I'll try harder" gets denied more often than "I'm enrolling in 12 credits instead of 18, meeting with a tutor weekly for MAT 265, and using the counseling center's biweekly check-in." If you don't have a concrete plan yet, that's exactly what an advising appointment is for — book one before writing the appeal, not after.

## If the appeal is denied
You can typically sit out a term (suspension is usually one semester) and petition to return, or transfer credits from a community college during the suspension term to keep progress moving — talk to an advisor about which path protects your financial aid and catalog year.
