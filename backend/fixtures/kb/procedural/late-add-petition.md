# Late Add / Late Drop Petition

## When you need this
The add/drop window has closed and either: (a) you need to add a class you were mistakenly left out of (instructor permission, registration error), or (b) you need to drop a class without it becoming a W and without waiting for the withdrawal deadline.

## Steps
1. Get instructor and department approval first — most petitions are denied without it. Email the instructor explaining the situation and ask them to confirm approval in writing (a reply-all email is enough).
2. Submit the **Petition for Late Registration Changes** through your college's student services office — this is a form, not a My ASU self-service flow; each college hosts its own version, search "[your college] late registration petition."
3. Attach the instructor approval and a brief explanation of why the request is late (registration hold that was resolved late, advising error, documented emergency, etc. — "I forgot" is rarely approved).
4. Processing typically takes 1–2 weeks. You remain responsible for coursework/attendance in the class while the petition is pending if you're trying to add.

## What trips people up
- Petitions filed for reasons within the student's control (didn't check My ASU, missed the deadline knowingly) are usually denied — the petition exists for registration errors and documented emergencies, not for changing your mind late.
- This is different from a course withdrawal: a withdrawal is always available up to its own deadline and doesn't need a petition. Only use this form if you're trying to act *after* both the add/drop and withdrawal windows, or need a drop with no W recorded.
